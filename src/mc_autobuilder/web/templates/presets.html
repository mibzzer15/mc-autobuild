Presets

What to do to every station of a given type: expand to max level, a desired service state, a free hiring phase, and a shopping list of vehicles. Applied automatically right after a station of that type is built from the plan, and can also be re-applied to any existing station from its detail page (safe to re-run — only takes the actions still needed).

Not covered yet: station extensions and equipment purchase — never confirmed against a real account, so not automated (see docs/missionchief-api.md).
